A professional microsoldering laboratory offering component-level repair for iPhones, MacBooks, and Android devices. Every repair is performed under a trinocular stereo microscope using hot air rework stations, ultrasonic cleaners, and professional-grade BGA reballing equipment.

Specialisations include: NAND flash data recovery from physically damaged drives, GPU and CPU reballing on MacBook logic boards, iPhone charging circuit diagnosis and repair, water damage cleaning and pad restoration, and schematic-guided fault tracing across all generations of Apple silicon.

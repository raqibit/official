raqi-build is my personal portfolio — a living, breathing showcase of software engineering, visual design, and microsoldering expertise. Built on Next.js 15 App Router with a fully custom design system featuring spotlight effects, animated typography, scroll-driven reveals, and a cinematic dark-native aesthetic.

Every interaction is intentional: the hero section reacts to mouse movement, the projects grid shuffles on every visit, and the overlay previewer lets clients experience work interactively without leaving the page. No templates, no boilerplate — designed and coded from scratch.
